Instructions & Code of Conduct
Important: Once you click "Start Examination Now", the timer will start immediately and cannot be paused.
  • MCQ Format: All questions are multiple-choice with 4 options. Exactly one option is correct.

  • Automatic Submission: When the countdown timer reaches 00:00:00, your exam will be automatically submitted and evaluated on the server.
  • Anti-Cheating Policy: Switching browser tabs, minimizing the exam window, or attempting to open developer tools may be flagged as malpractice.
